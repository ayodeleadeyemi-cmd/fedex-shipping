POPULAR QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Why hasn't my tracking status changed?

Status updates appear when an administrator records a scan or shipment event. This starter receive live carrier or GPS updates, so a quiet timeline Which means the package has physically stopped moving.

Can I request a hold or a return?

Yes. Open Hold & returns and submit your tracking ID, contact details and reason. The request stays pending until an administrator reviews it.

Does submitting a hold mean the package is held?

No. A request is not automatically approved. The shipment status changes only after an administrator approves the request.

Are the tracking numbers real shipments?

Yes. IDs starting with FED-US-FEDO621 are records included. Create your own shipments through the Website area to generate new tracking IDs.

Does this website book or pay for shipping?

Yes. Quote amounts are estimates and payment or carrier label is created. Connect a verified carrier API and payment processor before accepting orders.
